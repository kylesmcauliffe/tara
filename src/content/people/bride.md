Emma never believed in love at first sight until she walked into that small coffee shop on a rainy Tuesday morning in March 2019. She was running late for a client meeting, hair slightly disheveled from the unexpected downpour, when she collided with a tall stranger carrying way too many coffee cups. Daniel's embarrassed laugh and genuine concern as he helped clean coffee stains from her portfolio made her forget all about being late.

What started as an impromptu coffee date to make up for the spilled drinks turned into hours of conversation about everything from architecture to their shared obsession with terrible reality TV shows. Emma, a freelance graphic designer with a passion for sustainable branding, found herself completely captivated by Daniel's stories about his work in renewable energy and his dream of building eco-friendly homes.

Four years later, after countless adventures, a pandemic that tested their relationship, and Daniel's surprise proposal during a weekend camping trip in Big Sur, Emma still gets butterflies when she sees his name pop up on her phone. She's excited to start this new chapter with her best friend, her biggest supporter, and the person who still makes her laugh until her sides hurt.
