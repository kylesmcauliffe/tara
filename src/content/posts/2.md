Planning a wedding can feel overwhelming, but breaking it down into manageable monthly tasks makes the process much smoother. Here's your complete timeline to ensure nothing gets forgotten.

## 12 Months Before

Start with the big decisions: set your budget, choose your date, and book your venue. These foundational choices will guide all your other decisions throughout the planning process.

## 9-10 Months Before

Book your key vendors - photographer, caterer, florist, and entertainment. The best professionals get booked quickly, especially for popular wedding dates.

## 6-8 Months Before

Send save-the-dates, start shopping for your dress, and finalize your guest list. This is also the perfect time to start thinking about your color scheme and overall theme.

## 4-5 Months Before

Order invitations, book transportation, and schedule menu tastings. Start thinking about ceremony details like music, readings, and who will officiate.

## 2-3 Months Before

Send invitations, finalize headcount with vendors, and confirm all details. This is when you'll do your final dress fitting and order wedding favors if you're having them.

## 1 Month Before

Confirm final details with all vendors, pick up your dress, and delegate day-of responsibilities to your wedding party. Most importantly, try to relax and enjoy this exciting time.

## Week Of

Pack for your honeymoon, confirm vendor arrival times, and give your final headcount to the caterer. Focus on staying calm and getting plenty of rest.

---

Remember, this timeline is flexible. Adjust it based on your specific needs and don't stress if you're behind on a few things - your wedding will be beautiful regardless.
