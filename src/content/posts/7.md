A well-planned timeline is the secret to a stress-free wedding day. It ensures you have enough time for each event, beautiful photos, and moments to actually enjoy your celebration.

## Start with Your Ceremony Time

Work backwards from your ceremony time to plan everything else. This is usually the most fixed element of your day, as it's often determined by venue availability.

## Allow Extra Time for Getting Ready

Hair and makeup always take longer than expected. Build in buffer time and consider the order in which your wedding party will get ready to avoid bottlenecks.

## Schedule a First Look

Seeing each other before the ceremony allows for couple portraits in better lighting and can help calm pre-ceremony nerves. It also frees up time after the ceremony.

## Plan Efficient Photo Sessions

Group family photos by families and have a shot list ready. Assign someone to help gather people quickly. Consider doing wedding party photos before the ceremony.

## Build in Transition Time

Allow 15-30 minutes between each major event for transitions, touch-ups, and unexpected delays. This prevents your day from feeling rushed.

## Consider Cocktail Hour Length

Standard cocktail hours are 60-90 minutes. This gives you time for couple portraits while keeping guests entertained but not over-served.

## End at a Reasonable Time

Consider your vendors' overtime charges and your guests' energy levels. Most wedding receptions end between 10 PM and midnight.

---

Share your timeline with all vendors and key wedding party members. Having everyone on the same schedule ensures your day flows smoothly and you can focus on celebrating.
