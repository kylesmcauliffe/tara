Your wedding day is filled with precious moments that deserve to be captured beautifully. Whether you're working with a professional photographer or want to help your guests take better photos, these tips will ensure your special day is documented perfectly.

## Choose the Right Lighting

Natural light creates the most flattering wedding photos. Schedule your ceremony during golden hour if possible, or choose venues with large windows for soft, romantic lighting throughout the day.

## Focus on Candid Moments

While posed portraits are important, the most treasured photos often capture spontaneous laughter, tears of joy, and intimate glances between you and your partner when you think no one is watching.

## Include Detail Shots

Don't forget to photograph the small details that make your wedding unique - your rings, bouquet, table settings, and decorations. These images help tell the complete story of your special day.

## Capture Different Perspectives

Mix wide shots of the entire ceremony with close-ups of emotional reactions. Get photos from different angles - the bride walking down the aisle, guests' faces during vows, and the couple from behind the altar.

## Document the Reception Energy

The reception is where personalities shine. Capture the first dance, toasts, cake cutting, and especially the dance floor energy when everyone lets loose and celebrates.

## Create a Shot List

Work with your photographer to create a list of must-have shots - family groups, bridal party photos, and specific moments that are important to you both.

## Embrace Imperfection

Sometimes the most beautiful wedding photos aren't perfectly composed. A child running through the ceremony, wind catching your veil, or laughter during formal photos often become the most cherished images.

---

Remember, the best wedding photos capture genuine emotion and joy. Focus on being present in each moment, and the beautiful memories will naturally unfold before the camera.
