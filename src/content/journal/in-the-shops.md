The Brentwood shop opened early this week for a soft reset of the floor. Knits moved forward, summer linen stayed for the warm afternoons we still get, and a few dresses found a quieter corner near the light.

If you’re nearby, stop in—we’re happy to talk through fit, fabric, and what travels well between seasons.
