- Pan-seared scallops with cauliflower puree
- Vegetarian option: Roasted beet and goat cheese salad

**Main Course**

- Herb-crusted beef tenderloin with red wine reduction
- Pan-roasted salmon with lemon herb butter
- Vegetarian option: Wild mushroom risotto

**Dessert**

- Individual chocolate lava cakes
- Fresh fruit tart (gluten-free option available)

Each course will be paired with carefully selected wines. Non-alcoholic pairings are also available upon request.
