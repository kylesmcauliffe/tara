Celebrate with us immediately following the ceremony! Our cocktail hour is the perfect opportunity to mingle, congratulate the happy couple, and enjoy delicious drinks and appetizers.

## Refreshments

We'll be serving a selection of:

- **Signature Cocktails**: Including our special "Emma & Daniel" signature drink
- **Wine & Beer**: Carefully curated selection of local and international options
- **Non-Alcoholic Options**: Sparkling cider, artisan sodas, and fresh juices
- **Appetizers**: Gourmet hors d'oeuvres and canapés

## Location

The cocktail hour takes place on the beautiful outdoor terrace, offering stunning views and a relaxed atmosphere. String lights and floral arrangements will create a magical ambiance as the sun begins to set.

## Entertainment

Enjoy live acoustic music while you mingle and take photos. This is also the perfect time for family photos and catching up with friends and relatives.

_Don't forget to sign our guest book during cocktail hour!_
