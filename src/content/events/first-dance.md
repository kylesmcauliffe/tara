Witness our first dance as husband and wife - a moment we've been dreaming about since we got engaged. We'll be dancing to a song that holds special meaning in our relationship.

## Our Song

We've chosen "At Last" by Etta James, the same song that was playing when we first met at the coffee shop three years ago. It feels perfect to begin our married life dancing to the melody that brought us together.

## What Happens Next

After our first dance, we invite:

1. **Parent Dances**: Emma will dance with her father, Daniel will dance with his mother
2. **Wedding Party Dance**: Our bridesmaids and groomsmen will join us
3. **Everyone Invited**: All guests are welcome to join us on the dance floor

## Photography

Our photographers will capture this magical moment from multiple angles. We encourage guests to put away their phones during the first dance to be fully present with us.

## The Dance Floor

The dance floor will be surrounded by candlelight and twinkling lights, creating an intimate atmosphere for this special moment. The space will be cleared and spotlighted just for us.

_This is the moment we officially begin our journey as dance partners for life._
